How sharing works

Consent-based, start to finish.

Nobody appears on your dashboard without agreeing to it first, and health data never moves without both people saying yes.

Invite someone

Send a single-use link to a parent, a sibling or a friend. It expires if it is not used.

Their choice

Sleep, activity, stress — each one is opt-in, on their device, in plain language.

Both sides see the same

Sharing is mutual by design. Either of you can pause or end it, and the data stops immediately.

The full detail — every field we read, every provider we use, and how to delete everything — is in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
